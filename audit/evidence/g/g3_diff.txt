"""The single definition of region reading order.

Used by data preparation (to concatenate Azure anchor text and ground-truth
regions into page strings) and by evaluation (to reassemble per-region model
outputs into a page). Nothing else in the codebase may sort regions.

If the two paths ordered regions differently, alignment would still run and
page scoring would still produce a number; both would just be systematically
wrong. Hence one function, imported from both places.

Algorithm: recursive XY-cut over axis-aligned bounding boxes. At each level,
find every whitespace gap that no box crosses, horizontally (rows) and
vertically (columns); cut along whichever direction has the widest gap
(horizontal wins ties), recurse into the pieces. When no gap exists, fall back
to (top, left). Deterministic: ties break on the input index.
"""
